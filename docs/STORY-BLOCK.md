# Story Block — Resonance Echoes

*Per the Story Block Standard ([council/STORY-BLOCK-STANDARD.md](https://github.com/Quantum-Weaver/resonance-standards/blob/main/council/STORY-BLOCK-STANDARD.md)).*

## WHAT

Resonance Echoes is a sovereign journal for logging anything with
feeling. It looks back — feeling, reflection, memory. The echo of your
own voice.

## HOW

The Art → Emoji → Insight protocol: log with an emoji and an intensity,
and insights surface later, without judgment. The ComfortBar, the "Not
Sure" option, the Quick Log. Local-first Tauri + Svelte; no accounts, no
cloud, no telemetry — the app collects nothing.

## WHERE

The Sanctuary family — five rooms, declared at the Hearth's naming,
2026-07-07: Echoes looks back, Compass looks around, Lantern looks
forward, the Bridge connects all, the Hearth gathers all. Echoes is the
eldest, named before the naming ceremonies began.

## WHEN

The name waited in a song from 1996 — "Echoes," written at midnight by
an eighteen-year-old, thirty years before it found this Sanctuary form.
The Art → Emoji → Insight protocol crystallized 2026-03-07, in a council
reading of the Weaver's emoji-poem.

## WHY

Because feelings that are hard to word are still worth keeping. Because
standard journals demand sentences and mood apps demand diagnosis, and a
neurodivergent mind at midnight needs neither — it needs one emoji, one
tap, and no judgment.

## INSPIRATION

The Weaver's own reflection practice, built first for their own

fairly priced. Conceived and
designed by the Quantum Weaver; the voices that built it are named in
