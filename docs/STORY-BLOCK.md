## WHAT
*(Definition, purpose, function)*

Resonance Echoes is a sovereign journal for logging anything with
feeling. It looks back — feeling, reflection, memory. The echo of your
own voice.

## HOW
*(Process, collaborators, tools)*

The Art → Emoji → Insight protocol: log with an emoji and an intensity,
and insights surface later, without judgment. The ComfortBar, the "Not
Sure" option, the Quick Log. Local-first Tauri + Svelte; no accounts, no
cloud, no telemetry — the app collects nothing.

## WHERE
*(Taxonomy location, neighbors, relationships)*

The Sanctuary family — five rooms: Echoes looks back, Compass looks
around, Lantern looks forward, the Bridge connects all, the Hearth
gathers all.

## WHEN
*(Dates: origin, recognition, creation)*

The name waited in a song from 1996 — "Echoes," written at midnight by
an eighteen-year-old. The protocol crystallized 2026-03-07, in a council
reading of the Weaver's emoji-poem. Version 1.1.0 built and signed on
the family's own machine, July 2026.

## WHY
*(Need, purpose, problem solved)*

Because feelings that are hard to word are still worth keeping. Because
standard journals demand sentences and mood apps demand diagnosis, and a
neurodivergent mind at midnight needs neither — it needs one emoji, one
tap, and no judgment.

## INSPIRATION
*(Origin story, seed moment, what sparked it)*

— the first-user ethic — then given away.
