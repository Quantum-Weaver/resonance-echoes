## Full description
Resonance Echoes is a journal for moments — log anything you noticed or
felt as an "echo": a name, a sense, an emoji, an intensity, a note if
words are willing. That's the whole gesture. Two taps if that's all
today has in it.

Built neurodivergent-first:
• Gentle insights, never judgments — patterns shown as invitations, not
  scores. Streaks reset quietly. Nothing shames you.
• A "Not Sure" option everywhere — uncertainty is a valid entry.
• Progressive disclosure — the form starts tiny and grows only when you
  are ready.
• A timer that waits with you — pause it and the sand holds still;
  choose its chime (a rise, a bell, a drop, a pulse — all gentle by
  design) and how softly it speaks; and it sits with you in
  visualizations (an hourglass of sand, a breathing square, a mandala)
  while it runs.

Sovereign by architecture, not policy:
• Data collected: none. No account, no cloud, no analytics, ever.
• Everything lives in a local database on your device.
• Export your entire journal — echoes and your own emoji meanings
  together — as one open JSON file, and import it back any time.
• The purge truly purges — double-confirmed, complete, final, and it
  covers exactly the same ground export does.

From AudHDities: tools built for one neurodivergent family first,
then offered to everyone, free or fairly priced, so that no one is
exploited or manipulated over something they love.

## Commercial seller status, in order

Galaxy Store distributes free or paid apps only from an account with
commercial seller status. Themes sell through the same account; a
themes-only seller says so in the application.

1. **Four things settled before the portal, all matching each other:** the
   legal business name; the representative, a person's legal name exactly as
   on government ID; the business address, which is shown on the store
   listing (the house ward stands: never a home address); a PayPal or bank
   account in the business name, in the United States.
2. **D-U-N-S number** — free for the United States at
   https://www.dnb.com/duns/get-a-duns.html. Business name, address, Key
   Principal (the representative) and industry (software / app publishing)
   complete, in standard English characters. A profile change takes 2–5
   business days; resubmit to Samsung 5–10 business days after a change.
3. **Seller Portal registration** at https://seller.samsungapps.com — a
   Samsung account on a company-domain email (a public domain such as gmail
   or proton needs an explanation in the application); Corporate Seller;
   country United States, which cannot be changed after registration and
   must match the bank's country; first and last name exactly as on the ID.
4. **Request Corporate Commercial Seller** — the pop-up at sign-in, the
   button at the top right of the main screen, or Profile → Seller Status.
   Enter the D-U-N-S number and the financial information, and state that
   the account will sell apps and themes.
5. **Wait.** Up to 10 business days for the D-U-N-S check and up to 10 for
   the bank check. After 2–3 weeks with no answer: Help → Contact us →
   Contact us at the Customer Center.
6. **A rejection email names the cause.** The causes Samsung's pages name:
   a D-U-N-S industry that does not match app publishing; seller-account
   fields that differ from the D-U-N-S profile; a public-domain email; a
   bank in another country or another name; the same D-U-N-S on two Seller
   Portal accounts. Fix at the source, wait the 5–10 days, resubmit from the
   same account.

*Galaxy Themes designers are admitted separately: a portfolio of at least
three mockups, each with six screens (Lock, Home, Messages, Dialer,
Contacts, Settings) and eight redesigned app icons, at the Starter Kit's
size, as one PDF of original art, submitted through
https://developer.samsung.com/galaxy-themes while its submission window is
open. Designers are recruited every two years; review takes 4–8 weeks after
the window closes; approval opens Themes Studio and two years of publishing.*
